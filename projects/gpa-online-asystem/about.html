Welcome To Government Polytechnic Atraulia Azamgarh

Government Polytechnic Atraulia, located in Azamgarh, Uttar Pradesh, is a premier institution dedicated to technical education and skill development. Established with the vision of promoting technical proficiency among students, the college has become a vital part of the region's educational landscape.

Courses Offered

The polytechnic offers a range of diploma programs in various disciplines, including:


1. Computer Science and Engineering
2. Electrical Engineering
3. Mechanical Engineering
4. P.G Diploma in Computer Application.

Principal's About

Dr. Subodh Kant is a distinguished academician and visionary leader with over 14 years of expertise in technical education. Holding a Ph.D. in Computer Science and Engineering, he currently serves as the Principal of Government Polytechnic Atraulia, Azamgarh, and Director at AICCEDS. Dr. Kant has been instrumental in transforming technical education in Uttar Pradesh by prioritizing infrastructure development, digital governance, and robust industry-academia collaboration. His administrative leadership is marked by progressive curriculum reforms and the establishment of modern laboratories to bridge the gap between classroom learning and industry demands.

"Education is the most powerful weapon which you can use to change the world. Technology amplifies that power."

Dr. Subodh Kant
Principal, Goverment Polytechnic Atraulia
